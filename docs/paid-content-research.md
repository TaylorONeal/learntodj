# Paid practice-material boundary — DJ-05

Status: desk research and interview proposal, September 16, 2026. LearnToDJ is **not confirmed permanently free**. No price, checkout, subscription, account requirement, entitlement, purchase button, or content restriction is activated. Existing practice and guides remain unchanged in availability.

## Observed alternatives and limits

- [Crossfader's Complete DJ Package](https://wearecrossfader.co.uk/complete-dj) advertises a broad course bundle with lifetime access, equipment-specific courses, a transitions library, and instructor support. This is evidence of a structured learning offer, not proof of its sales or of demand for our much smaller practice tool.
- [Digital DJ Tips' Access All Areas](https://www.digitaldjtips.com/access-all-areas-benefits/) describes an extensive DJ/production course library with lifetime learning access. Again, scope and instructor value differ substantially from nine recall questions.
- Crossfader also offers [free beginner preview material](https://wearecrossfader.co.uk/blog/the-complete-dj-course-free/). A paid pack competes with useful free instruction as well as courses; simply adding more quiz questions may not justify a purchase.

These are provider descriptions checked for offer structure, not independently verified outcomes, endorsements, or a comprehensive market study. Do not copy lessons, footage, sound packs, or testimonials. No competitor price is used as a validated willingness-to-pay estimate for LearnToDJ. Recheck current terms before a later comparison.

Inference to test: a narrowly scoped, educator-reviewed practice pack may help learners move from recall to a repeatable hands-on exercise without buying a large course. Whether anyone values that enough to pay remains unknown.

## Interview stimulus — not available for sale

Working concept: **Phrase & Transition Practice Pack**, proposed one-time $9–19 research range. Test $9, $14, and $19 as separate interview prompts, not a published price, discount, or purchase commitment. Do not describe the existing app as a trial or remove existing content to create artificial demand.

Proposed deliverables to author and review before any sale:

- Six structured practice cards spanning phrase entry, cueing, bass handoff, gain, recovery and exit timing; each states setup, action, what to listen for, and common errors.
- Eighteen new conceptual/transfer questions with explanations and a mapping to the cards; avoid cosmetic rewordings of the nine existing questions.
- Six original diagrams or silent demonstrations. Audio examples are optional and require owned/licensed composition, recording and distribution rights; no commercial track library is assumed.
- One printable session worksheet and an instructor-reviewed explanation key, with version/date and corrections route.
- A clear statement of prerequisites and limits: no live feedback, personalized performance grading, certification, or promised improvement.

## Content and rights register required before implementation

For each item record author, reviewer, factual/source basis, version, accessibility text/captions, license owner, permitted platforms/territories, redistribution terms and any expiry. Unapproved rows cannot ship. Reviewer time is a cost; “reviewed” cannot mean only that automated tests passed. Product-owned artwork can be reused; private contacts and contracts stay outside git.

## Delivery work before payment activation

1. Decide whether a downloadable pack or native digital-content entitlement best fits observed learner needs. No provider, account or identifier is selected here.
2. Complete content and learner review first. Define exactly which items/version the buyer receives and what future updates, support, refund handling and access duration mean; make no lifetime-content-production promise by default.
3. Review applicable platform purchase/delivery rules for the chosen distribution at implementation time. Do not route around store rules or assume one payment flow fits every platform.
4. Verify paid success → correct access, cancelled/failed/pending/duplicate payment → honest state, reinstall/restore on the supported platform, offline availability, refunds/revocation, and support recovery. Signed native tests are required for a native offer.
5. Preserve existing local progress and access. If an account ever becomes necessary, it requires a separate privacy/auth decision rather than speculative implementation now.
6. Only after value, rights, delivery, support and publisher decisions: prepare a concrete provider integration proposal. This research does not authorize activating it.

A $3–6/month subscription remains a later interview option only if sustained use and funded ongoing reviewed content justify it. Educator licensing depends on observed class use and explicit permitted reuse, not a rebranded consumer pack.

## Transparent cost sensitivity — illustrative USD, not a quote

Assumptions for discussion: 12 authoring hours × $30 = $360; four educator-review hours × $60 = $240; fixed preparation cost $600. Assume a combined 20% allowance for platform/payment fees and refunds, $0.25 variable delivery cost, and five support minutes × $30/hour = $2.50 per buyer. None of these rates, fees or demand numbers is validated. Taxes, acquisition, ongoing updates and overhead are excluded and would lower contribution further.

Contribution before excluded costs = `price × 0.80 − 0.25 − 2.50`.

| Hypothetical price | Contribution per buyer | Buyers to recover $600 fixed cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $9 | $4.45 | 135 |
| $14 | $8.45 | 72 |
| $19 | $12.45 | 49 |

This is arithmetic sensitivity, not revenue forecast or a promise of profitability. Replace assumptions with actual reviewer quotes, chosen-channel fees, support observations and refund experience before a commercial decision. No spending was made or authorized.

## Decision after research

Use the [learner pilot](learner-pilot.md): record current alternatives, missing value, price objections, specific next practice opportunity, and willingness to evaluate a completed sample. Do not label hypothetical agreement a sale. If the existing concept explanations are confusing, fix them before authoring a paid pack. If a reviewed sample is useful but the delivery/reuse need is unclear, continue interviews rather than adding subscription machinery.
