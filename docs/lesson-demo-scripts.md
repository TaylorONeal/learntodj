## DJ-C01 — When should the next track enter? (DJ-03)

Hypothesis: a specific phrasing decision makes the learning task understandable before opening the app.

- 0–3s, question screen (`02-question.png`): “Your next track is cued. When would you bring it in?”
- 3–8s, hold the three real options: “Choose a musical phrase, a loud waveform, or the middle of a vocal line.”
- 8–18s, tap the actual phrase answer, show explanation (`03-explanation.png`): “Starting on beat one of a new phrase helps musical changes line up. Listen for the structure; don't rely only on loudness.”
- 18–25s, related guide: “LearnToDJ gives you a short conceptual question and an explanation. It doesn't listen to or grade your mix.”
- 25–30s, app result: “Try a practice round.” Add a destination only after it is verified and publication approved.

Caption draft: “One phrasing decision to think through before your next hands-on practice session. Concept practice in LearnToDJ; not live audio feedback.”

Alt text: “A phrasing question followed by an explanation about bringing in a track at the start of a musical phrase.”

Review: confirm phrasing advice is a starting point, not an absolute rule for every track. Evidence: `src/data/practice.ts`, question `phrase`. No observed learning result or professional-performance promise.

## DJ-C03 — Why more bass may be the wrong answer (DJ-03)

Hypothesis: a useful explanation for a wrong answer demonstrates value without promising automated coaching.

- 0–4s, second question after a real first answer: “Both tracks are playing and the bass sounds muddy. Would you boost both LOW knobs?”
- 4–10s, deliberately tap that actual wrong answer. Disclose on-screen/narration: “Demonstration: choosing a wrong answer.”
- 10–22s, show the app's real feedback: “The lesson suggests giving one track the low end at a time. A controlled bass swap can keep the kick and bass clearer.”
- 22–28s: “This is a concept to test by ear on your own equipment. LearnToDJ isn't analyzing your audio.”
- 28–32s: “Try a practice round.”

Caption draft: “A wrong answer can be a useful practice prompt. Review the explanation, then listen for yourself during hands-on practice.”

Alt text: “The EQ question explains a controlled bass swap after a deliberately incorrect answer in a demo.”
